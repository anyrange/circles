# Yu, Alegroth, Chatzipetrou, and Gorschek (2023) -- Automated NFR Testing in Continuous Integration Environments: A Multi-Case Study of Nordic Companies

**File:** `sources/s10664-023-10356-1.pdf`
**Venue:** Empirical Software Engineering, 2023
**BibTeX key:** `yu2023nfrci`
**DOI:** `10.1007/s10664-023-10356-1`

## What It Says

- Studies how companies enable and support automated non-functional requirement testing in CI environments.
- Shows that CI environments provide data, tools, and components that support maintainability, reliability, performance, security, and scalability evaluation.
- Describes CI primarily as an environment composed of tools and components, not only as a workflow script.
- Supports the view that test behavior depends on the surrounding execution environment and infrastructure.

## Key Quotes

> "automated testing shows the potential to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of quality assurance, especially in the context of Continuous Integration (CI)" (p. 1)

> "studies on how companies manage automated NFR testing through CI are limited" (p. 1)

> "data produced by CI environments can be used for evaluation and monitoring of implemented NFR quality" (p. 1)

## How to Use in Paper

- **Related Work:** Support the discussion of CI environments as enabling infrastructure for automated testing.
- **Evaluation:** Help justify the focus on CI pipeline structure and shared tooling components.
- **Discussion:** Contrast general CI support for testing with the narrower problem of uniform package-level test environments in a monorepo.
