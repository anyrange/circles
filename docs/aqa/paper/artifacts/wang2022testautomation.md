# Wang, Mantyla, Liu, and Markkula (2022) -- Test Automation Maturity Improves Product Quality: Quantitative Study of Open Source Projects Using Continuous Integration

**File:** `sources/wang2022.pdf`
**Venue:** Journal of Systems and Software, 2022
**BibTeX key:** `wang2022testautomation`
**DOI:** `10.1016/j.jss.2022.111259`

## What It Says

- Examines how test automation maturity affects product quality, effort, and release cycle in CI contexts.
- Reports that higher test automation maturity is associated with higher product quality and shorter release cycles.
- Treats test automation as a core dependency of successful CI rather than an optional add-on.
- Supports the importance of reliable and repeatable automated test execution, but does not address cross-package environment consistency inside monorepos.

## Key Quotes

> "The ability of CI to deliver quality at speed depends on reliable test automation" (p. 1)

> "test automation became the backbone of CI" (p. 1)

> "higher levels of test automation maturity are positively associated with higher product quality" (p. 1)

## How to Use in Paper

- **Related Work:** Support the argument that stable automated testing infrastructure matters for software quality.
- **Evaluation:** Help justify why CI pipeline uniformity is a relevant evaluation dimension.
- **Discussion:** Clarify that automation maturity and toolchain unification are related but distinct concerns.
