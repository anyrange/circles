# Hilton et al. (2016) -- Usage, Costs, and Benefits of Continuous Integration in Open-Source Projects

**File:** `sources/HiltonETAL16ContinuousIntegration.pdf`
**Venue:** ASE 2016
**BibTeX key:** `hilton2016ci`

## What It Says

- CI systems automate compilation, building, and testing.
- CI adoption was already widespread in open-source practice and continued to grow.
- The paper studies both the costs and the organizational benefits of CI across a large project sample.
- CI is important infrastructure for automation, but the study focuses on service usage and workflow effects rather than local toolchain uniformity.

## Key Quotes

> "CI systems automate the compilation, building, and testing of software." (p. 426)

> "CI is widely used" (p. 427)

## How to Use in Paper

- **Related Work:** Show that automated testing infrastructure matters, while distinguishing CI orchestration from local test-environment consistency.
- **Evaluation:** Support the use of CI pipeline structure as one evaluation dimension in the case study.
