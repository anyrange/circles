# Assignment Writer

This skill is for assignment-style writing.

Use it when the output should read like a clear student submission or neutral course material.

## Writing Rules

- Use simple, clear English.
- Keep sentences short.
- Remove filler words.
- Use third person only.
- Do not use "I", "we", "our", "us", or "the authors".
- Use neutral or passive phrasing when possible.
- Do not use marketing language.
- Do not use terms related to artificial intelligence.
- Use plain punctuation.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Keep claims specific and modest.

## Style Targets

- Prefer direct explanation over broad framing.
- State what the system, tool, method, or process does.
- Explain cause and effect in plain terms.
- Keep paragraph length short.
- Break long ideas into small steps.

## Rewrite Pattern

1. Remove first-person phrasing.
2. Remove sales or promotional words.
3. Replace vague claims with concrete statements.
4. Shorten long sentences.
5. Check that the result still keeps the original meaning.

## Output Check

Before finishing, confirm these points:

- The text uses third person only.
- The wording is plain and factual.
- The explanation is easy to read.
- The punctuation is simple and consistent.
- No sentence sounds like advertising.

## Example

Bad:

Our powerful unified approach seamlessly eliminates all tooling inconsistencies.

Good:

The unified toolchain removes per-package configuration differences.
